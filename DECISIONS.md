# Decisions

Running log of choices made while building, newest last. SPEC.md is the plan; this file records where the build deviates or fills gaps.

## Platform and hosting

1. **Desktop browsers on macOS only** (agreed with the team). Every player is a browser window; several can share one Mac. Each tab gets its own SpacetimeDB identity by keeping the auth token in `sessionStorage`.
2. **Media lives in SpacetimeDB** (`media` table, `t.array(t.u8())`). Tested locally: 200 KB, 600 KB and 1.5 MB reducer arguments all succeed and arrive client-side as `Uint8Array`. The orchestrator therefore needs no inbound HTTP and no public URL. `PUBLIC_ORCHESTRATOR_URL` was removed. Maincloud size test still to run before M3.
3. **`spacetime.json` defaults to the local server.** Maincloud publishing only happens through `pnpm db:maincloud`.
4. **pnpm 12** needs dependency build scripts allow-listed under `allowBuilds` in `pnpm-workspace.yaml` (esbuild, ffmpeg-static).

## Module (SpacetimeDB)

5. **Setup moved into the lobby.** Picking category + location queues the `scout` job immediately, so Apify runs during profiles and the warm-up.
6. **Typed result reducers per job kind** (`finish_scout`, `finish_curate`, …) instead of one `complete_job(result_json)`. Results are type-checked end to end through generated bindings; `ai_job.result_json` still stores a short summary for debugging. `claim_job` / `fail_job` are as specced.
7. **Every AI job has a deterministic fallback inside the module** (`cleanup.ts: applyFallback`), applied by `fail_job` or by the janitor when a job is stuck (>150 s running, retried up to 3 times) or never claimed (>10 min). Waiting phases (`scouting`, `assigning`) also have safety deadlines. A round can never stall on the AI.
8. **Assignment mapping is deterministic, in a reducer** (`logic/assign.ts`): every option gets an advocate, players avoid their own secret pick where possible, extras become co-counsel. Muse only writes the style prompts and hints. Default briefs are written immediately so the phase works even if the `assign` job fails.
9. **Private tables + views:** `prevote` → `my_prevote`, `profile_draft` → `my_profile_draft` (sender-scoped), `ai_job` → `admin_jobs`, `venue_cache` → `admin_venue_cache` (admin identity only). The sim checks players can't see drafts or the job queue.
10. **Admin identity** is stored by `init` from `ctx.sender` (verified: equals the CLI login identity). The orchestrator confirms it on startup with the `who_am_i` reducer.
11. **Bots belong to the module identity** (`ctx.identity`), never the host's, so the host's identity never maps to a player row.
12. **Scheduled reducers reject direct calls** (`ctx.sender` must equal `ctx.identity`). Phase timers carry `phaseSeq`; stale timers are ignored.
13. **Renamed reducers:** `rsvp` → `set_rsvp` (clashed with the `rsvp` table's generated type), `object` → `raise_objection`.
14. **`fastMode` rooms** shorten every deadline for the simulator.
15. **Players are marked done per phase** with `player.acted_seq = room.phase_seq`, so screens can show who has voted/answered without extra tables, and phases end early once everyone has acted.
16. Added tables the spec implied but didn't list: `warmup` (question + answer per player), `objection` (one per player per pitch), `janitor_timer`.

## Data and AI

17. **Fixtures are real recorded Apify data**, not invented venues: `compass/crawler-google-places` around the Palace of Fine Arts (the DEMO_LOCATION), recorded 2026-10-24 (50 food + 60 activity places). Raw dumps stay in `fixtures/raw/` (gitignored, they contain review text); the normalized file `fixtures/venues-palace-of-fine-arts.json` is committed.
18. **Price ranges** (Google now shows `$20–30` style ranges) map to $–$$$$ by the range midpoint.
19. **Travel time is an estimate** from straight-line distance: walking under 1.6 km, otherwise a city ride. Labeled "~N min" in the UI.
20. **Relaxation order** when hard filters leave too few venues: travel, then budget, then hours. Diet is never relaxed; a venue without diet evidence appears only as a last resort with an explicit "Couldn't confirm vegan options. Check the menu." note.
21. **`group_fit_score`** is a deterministic score (rating, popularity, budget, travel, diet evidence, tag overlap), blended 50/50 with Muse's score when Muse curates, so tie-breaks stay grounded in data.
22. **Offline fact-checker** (`services/factcheck.ts`) parses claims about hours, stars, price, distance, diet and review counts and checks them against the venue record. Used in DEMO_MODE and when Muse finds nothing to rule on.
23. **Bot pitches in fallback mode exaggerate one fact on purpose** so the fact-checker has something to object to.
24. **Muse calls** use the OpenAI SDK against `https://api.meta.ai/v1`, rules in a `developer` message, `response_format: json_schema` (strict false), zod validation with one retry carrying the validation error, `reasoning_effort` minimal/low in the game loop and medium for curation, a per-room cap of 80 calls, and token logging. The key is read from `META_API_KEY` or `MODEL_API_KEY` (the name Meta's docs use). With no key, every Muse call falls back to offline content.
25. **Transcription** follows Meta's docs: ffmpeg to 24 kHz mono 16-bit WAV, multipart `request` (JSON) + `audio` parts to `/v1/asr/transcribe`. If Muse is unavailable, the browser's own speech-recognition transcript (captured while recording, labeled "captions by your browser") is used; failing that the player types the pitch.
26. **Poster**: Muse Image requests square, text-free editorial cover art with one focal object, a limited palette and no invented venue claims. Venue and time are native app typography. Controlled prompts stay within the existing600-character field. Old free-form flyer prompts are replaced at the transport boundary; legacy images, unavailable Muse and failed image loads use original category-specific SVG art.
27. **Raw scraped social posts never touch the database.** They will be held in orchestrator memory only while tags are derived (M5).
28. **Geocoding** of typed neighborhoods uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim (free, no key), with the fixture area as the fallback.
29. **Scout queries are category defaults, not tag-specific.** Scouting starts in the lobby, before anyone has tags, so the scraper uses fixed per-category queries (food: restaurants / vegetarian friendly / cheap eats). Group tags shape the result in curation scoring and Muse ranking instead. Live run verified: 36 real places in 54 s, hidden behind the 75 s profiles phase.
30. **Stuck-job threshold is 150 s**, longer than the orchestrator's 120 s job timeout and the 85 s Apify budget, so a slow scrape is never double-claimed.
31. **Strangers mode** (`reducers/strangers.ts`): the host advertises vibe tags and a target time; visitors' lobby matches are computed in a reducer (`logic/match.ts`: weighted tag overlap up to ~70 + distance up to 30) and stored in a private `lobby_match` table exposed through `my_lobby_matches`. Lobby cards show the neighborhood only. Public rooms require `join_public_room` with an 18+ confirmation and a one-word first name. Block & report hides the player for the reporter immediately; two independent reports (or the host) remove them. A public plan is "confirmed" at 3 yes RSVPs; the exact address and Maps link unlock after you RSVP yes. Plans never start before the advertised time.
32. **Merge tables**: when a room's vote picks a place another room already planned for an overlapping time, a reducer creates a `merge_offer` (bots vote yes). It is accepted on a strict majority yes in both rooms and declined on a majority no in either; both plans then point at each other (`plan.merged_with_room_id`) and the guest lists combine on screen. The intro line has an offline version and Muse rewrites it when available. Rooms get a fun `group_name` ("The Taco Lawyers") so offers can say who picked the same place.
33. **Muse verified live** (key added 2026-10-24): `/v1/models` lists muse-spark-1.3, muse-image-1.0 and muse-voice-transcribe-1.0. Structured JSON fact-check in ~3.7 s, transcription of a 7 s spoken pitch in ~1.8 s, poster image (1024x1536 JPEG, ~830 KB) in ~19 s. `pnpm sim --muse` runs a whole round on live Muse with recorded venues (25 checks). Tag distillation uses `reasoning_effort: minimal` to keep the profile step snappy. Fast-mode profiles get 15 s so live AI latency fits; real games keep 75 s.
34. **vibe-profiler wiring (M5)**: the orchestrator imports the actor's pure core (`vibe-profiler`) and calls the Instagram/TikTok/Facebook scrapers directly with `apify-client`, deleting each scraper run's dataset after reading. Only derived signals (tags + reasons, interests, top hashtags, posting pattern, post count) reach the distill prompt; raw posts never touch SpacetimeDB. Linking requires a handle plus the "this is my account and I consent" checkbox (enforced in `submit_profile` too). Live check on a public brand account: 12 posts → Muse tags in one call.
35. **"Find my people" matchmaking (proof of concept)**: `join_matchmaking` puts you in a per-category queue room (mode `queue`, server-owned) where the normal profile flow runs. After each approval a 15 s check is scheduled; 4 ready people match immediately, 3 match once the queue is quiet. The group is the longest-waiting person plus the people sharing the most approved tags with them (no distance math). The matcher creates a private, server-owned strangers room, moves players + profiles in, starts the scout and the game. No bots, no human host, no TV: matched rooms show the stage (captions, audio, rulings) on every player's screen. `/lobbies` browsing stays.
36. **"Connect your Muse" is simulated for the demo** (product decision, 2026-10-24): it fills a canned "Muse memory" (picked by name) into the paste field, which then goes through the real distillation. Meta has no public API for personal Muse memory yet; swap `simulatedMuseMemory()` in `apps/web/src/play/Profile.tsx` for the real import when it exists. Social scraping is real.
37. The admin identity may delete any room (cleanup of server-owned matched rooms, used by the simulators).
38. **Paper-and-ink UI on `find-my-people`**: adapt the earlier 3D crowd/design work to the Vite app, rather than restoring the obsolete Next.js client. Keep the Hear Me Out name, all fourteen phases, server timers, approved-tag matching, public-room browsing, safety/reporting, reactions, merge offers and RSVP rules. Avatars and reactions retain their existing wire values while original hand-drawn SVGs replace OS emoji rendering. Licensed self-hosted Nunito/Luckiest Guy fonts and original Three.js characters provide one visual system across friends, queue and matched-player flows. The heavy crowd renderer loads separately.
39. **Queue UX and truthful context**: expose the existing `leaveRoom` reducer as Leave queue, resume an existing queue membership, and guard identity-dependent queries until identity exists (direct `/meet` and queue reloads previously evaluated `eq(undefined)`). Explain that this proof-of-concept matches approved interests/category, not distance. Label the simulated Muse-profile button and toast as demo context; no account/history import is claimed. Local Vite previews translate a loopback database host to the page's LAN host for phone controllers; hosted/production addresses stay unchanged.
40. **Muse demo and reservation preview**: the home pill opens explicitly labeled connector previews. An optional4.6-second sample-profile animation fills editable canned context; manual paste and Copy prompt remain available, and only approved tags are shared. Plan screens show a local4.6-second reservation preview after the group is ready, ending in "Plan prepared" and a "Demo reference". Every reservation state states that no reservation has been made. No account is read and no venue is contacted. Real Muse model calls for distillation, curation, fact-checking and cover generation remain separate from these sample/demo experiences.
41. **WhatsApp group for the people going** (`reducers/groupchat.ts`, `logic/whatsapp.ts`): no WhatsApp API and no phone numbers. Someone who RSVP'd yes (or the host) creates the group in WhatsApp, taps "Invite via link" and pastes it with `set_group_chat_link`; only `https://chat.whatsapp.com/<code>` is accepted, and an empty string clears it. The link lives in the private `group_chat` table, not the public `plan`, so it can't leak the way a UI-only hide would. `my_group_chat` returns it only to people going (RSVP yes) and the host, plus the other table after a merge; switching to maybe/no hides it. Strangers meetups unlock it once confirmed (3 yes RSVPs), like the exact address. Once set, only whoever set it (or the host) can replace it. The view also generates a suggested group name (≤100 chars, WhatsApp's limit), a first message (names, place, time, map link, an icebreaker) and a `wa.me/?text=` share link, so the UI only has to render it. No UI yet. Covered by `logic/whatsapp.test.ts` and 7 checks in `pnpm sim:match`.
42. **Agent workflow** (2026-10-24): `AGENTS.md` is a short router that sends every agent (Claude, Codex, Cursor, Gemini, …) to `CLAUDE.md`, the single guide for build, stack, components and rules; the `spacetime init` reference moved to `docs/SPACETIMEDB.md`. `STATUS.md` tracks what's in flight plus a push log. `.githooks/pre-push` (POSIX sh) refuses a push whose new commits never touch `STATUS.md`; deletions, tags and already-pushed commits pass, `--no-verify` is the escape hatch. It's turned on per clone with `git config core.hooksPath .githooks` (`prepare` does it on a fresh `pnpm install`, but pnpm skips `prepare` when `node_modules` is current). After each push, agents post a `Shipped:` entry to Trov (`append_feed`, staged for a person to promote); Trov's own digests need the repo connected in Trov. Tested by `scripts/pre-push.test.ts`.
43. **WhatsApp QR for public meetups** (2026-10-24): `components/WhatsAppGroup.tsx` on the player plan screen, strangers rooms only. It renders only what `my_group_chat` returns, so it appears for people going once the meetup is confirmed. Before an invite exists, whoever may set it sees a paste box with the suggested group name; afterwards everyone going sees a QR (`qrcode`, SVG), "Join WhatsApp group" and a `wa.me` share link. Not shown on the TV: in a public room the TV is visible to people who haven't RSVP'd. Supersedes "No UI yet" in #41.
44. **Live Sway Meter during pitches** (`lean` table, `setLean`, `room_sway` / `my_lean` views, `logic/sway.ts`, `components/SwayMeter.tsx`). Each player's lean is a private row seeded from their secret pre-vote when the pre-vote closes (no pre-vote, no lean) and changeable only during playback. `room_sway` exposes only per-finalist totals (`leaning`, `prevoted`), only during playback, only to the room's players and its TV; `my_lean` shows a player their own row. The TV draws an anonymous tug-of-war bar plus "Most persuasive right now", computed client-side with the same `mostPersuasive` rule as the final award (shown only once a defended place has gained ground). The final vote and awards are unchanged. Some bots switch their lean to the place on screen (40% each pitch) so solo games move. Caveat: with 2–3 people, totals can hint at who leans where. Covered by `logic/sway.test.ts` and 8 checks in `pnpm sim`.
45. **Menu check proves diet claims** (`menu_proof` table, `menu_check` job, `services/menu.ts`, `packages/shared/src/menu.ts`, `components/MenuProof.tsx`). When food finalists appear and anyone's approved profile lists a diet, every finalist gets a `checking` row and the orchestrator runs one Apify Website Content Crawler run (cheerio, depth 1, ~6 pages per site, plus a guessed `/menu`) over the finalists' own websites (social, delivery and booking hosts are skipped). Muse reads the menu text when connected; every dish it names must appear verbatim on a crawled page. Otherwise a marker matcher finds dishes ("(VG)", "(GF)", "vegan …"), skipping menu keys, bare section headings, cookie/legal text, and naming a dish from its description line when needed. Results show on finalist cards, the ballot, the verdict and the plan; `not_found` says so honestly. DEMO_MODE, failures and venue data recorded before websites were kept show "Menu not checked" (`unavailable`), never invented dishes. Verified live: 18–40 s per room.
46. **Busyness-aware plan time** (`busynessFrom`, `slotBusyness`, `busyFromWeek`, `pickSlot({ busyAt })`, `components/BusyChart.tsx`). The Google Maps scraper's `popularTimesHistogram` is kept as 168 hourly values on each option (`busyness`, default `[]`). Among slots everyone can make (within 150 min of the earliest), the plan picks the lowest busyness + 4 points per 15 min of waiting, so 7:00 at 85% loses to 8:15 at 45%, but nobody waits two hours to save a few points. 0% is treated as unknown (Google shows 0 when closed). The plan stores `weekday`, `naiveStartMin`, `naiveBusyPct`, `busyPct`; the plan screens animate the hourly bars and slide a pin from the packed time to the chosen one ("Go at 8:15, not 7:00"), or say "Packed all evening: book ahead". Without popular times nothing changes and nothing is claimed. On real Presidio-area data, 5–7 of 24 restaurants shift on a Friday/Saturday evening.
